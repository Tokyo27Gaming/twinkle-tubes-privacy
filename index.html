Privacy Policy — Twinkle Tubes: Ball Sort

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Publisher: TOKYO27GAMING
Privacy contact: tokyo27gaming@gmail.com

This policy describes privacy practices for the Android game Twinkle Tubes: Ball Sort. If this page is first published after October 21, 2026, update the effective date to the actual publication date before publishing.

Information stored by the game

The game does not require an account and the publisher does not operate a game server for player accounts, gameplay, or cloud sync. Puzzle progress, settings, statistics, daily challenge records, and hint/undo counts are stored in app storage on your device. The app asks the player to choose an age from 1 to 99 or skip. The exact age is checked on the device and is not saved or sent by the game. The app saves only an advertising age group: adult (18 or older) or child-safe (under 18 or skipped).

Android app backup is enabled. Depending on Android and device backup settings, some app data may be included in device backup. You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display banner ads, interstitial ads after certain completed levels, and optional rewarded ads for in-game hints or undos. Rewarded ads are not offered in child-safe mode. Ads are requested only when Google consent status and ad availability allow them.

The app also uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent information, show consent messages when required, and provide privacy options when required. Under-18 players and players who skip the age question receive child-safe ad treatment: the app sends child-directed and under-age-of-consent signals and limits ad content to a G rating. Players who choose age 18 or older use the adult ad mode, subject to applicable consent choices.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address, app interactions (including ad views and taps), diagnostics, and device or account identifiers to provide, measure, and protect advertising services. Google processes information under its own policies. See Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and Google UMP privacy and consent information.

Children

Twinkle Tubes: Ball Sort is designed for players of all ages. The game itself does not send the player's exact age or gameplay records to the publisher. In child-safe mode, rewarded ads are unavailable and ad requests use the child-safe settings described above. Google may still process information needed to provide, measure, secure, or manage advertising and consent, as described in Google's policies. Parents or guardians may contact the publisher with privacy questions.

Sharing

If you choose the game's share-result feature, the game provides result text to your device's sharing feature or clipboard. Any app or service you choose to receive that text handles it under its own privacy policy. The publisher does not receive the shared text on a game server.

Retention and choices

Local gameplay data remains in app storage until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup settings. Google handles information processed by its advertising and consent services under Google's retention practices and policies. Where Google requires it, the game provides a Privacy options control so you can revisit applicable consent choices. For questions or privacy requests, contact the publisher at the email above.

Security

The game stores gameplay data in app storage and does not operate a publisher game server. Advertising and consent traffic is handled by Google's SDKs. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, SDKs, or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published.

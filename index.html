Privacy Policy — Twinkle Tubes: Ball Sort

Effective date: October 23, 2026
Publisher: Christian Zammit, publishing as Tokyo27Gaming
Privacy contact: tokyo27gaming@gmail.com

This policy explains how the Android game Twinkle Tubes: Ball Sort handles information. The game does not require a player account, and the publisher does not operate a game server. The game does not display advertisements or include an advertising or consent-messaging SDK. Optional Gem pack purchases are offered through Google Play Billing.

Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, the current board, settings, statistics, Daily Challenge records, hints, undos, Gem balances, owned items, and extra-flask balances on your device. Gameplay information is stored in the app's local WebView storage; the Gem balance, owned items, and purchase records are stored in private Android app storage. The game does not send gameplay progress or Gem balances to a publisher-operated server.

At first launch, the game asks you to select an age from 1 to 99 to continue. This is a self-reported choice and does not independently verify age. The selected age is not saved or sent by the game; the app stores only that the age-selection screen was completed.

Android app backup is enabled. Depending on your Android device, account, and backup settings, some app data may be included in a device backup. Backup and restore behavior can vary. The publisher does not provide a cloud recovery service for game progress or Gem balances.

Google Play purchases

The game uses Google Play Billing for optional one-time Gem pack purchases. During a purchase, the app receives the product identifier, purchase state, and purchase token from Google Play. It stores a hash of a processed purchase token on your device to help prevent duplicate Gem delivery. The app does not send purchase tokens to a publisher-operated server. Google Play processes the payment and related purchase information under its own terms and privacy practices. The app does not receive your full payment-card details.

Gem packs are consumable purchases. The resulting Gem balance is stored on your device. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the game after a purchase has been consumed, the publisher may not be able to restore those Gems because there is no publisher account or purchase-restoration server. Google Play purchase records can be managed through your Google Play account, but they do not guarantee recovery of the locally stored Gem balance.

For more information, see Google Play Billing and Google's Privacy Policy.

Internet access and sharing

The game's puzzle content is bundled in the app. Network access is used for Google Play Billing. Privacy and support links open in the browser or email app you choose.

If you choose to share a puzzle result, Android passes the result text to the app or service you select. The publisher does not receive that text through a Twinkle Tubes game server.

Children and age selection

The game is intended for children and adults. The first-launch age screen is self-reported and is used only to confirm that the screen was completed; the selected age is not retained or sent. Parents or guardians with privacy questions may contact the publisher using the email address below. Optional purchases are presented and processed through Google Play.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains in app storage until it is cleared or the game is uninstalled, subject to Android backup settings. The publisher cannot retrieve or delete a locally stored game balance or progress from a server. Google Play handles payment records under its own account and retention settings.

Contacting the publisher

If you email the publisher, your email address and the information in your message will be received so the publisher can respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for your question.

Security and changes

Game progress and the Gem balance are stored on your device. The publisher does not operate a game server or backend purchase-verification service. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change; the effective date will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For game support, privacy questions, or privacy requests, contact Christian Zammit, publishing as Tokyo27Gaming, at tokyo27gaming@gmail.com.
